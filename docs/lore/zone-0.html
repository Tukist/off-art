Zone 0

Information

a.k.a. Joas

Music Empty Warehouse (original) Tender Sugar (original) A Secure Place for All Your Things (remaster) A Sure Place Away from Your Things (remaster) Le Dernier Petit Sucre (remaster) Zone 0, also known as Joas,[1] is the first area of the game that the The Player encounters and the first of the multiple Zones that exist within The Nothingness. The Batter is transported to Zone 0 from The Nothingness by some unknown entity,[2] afterwards meeting the Zone's guardian, the Judge.[3]. This Zone introduces the Player to basic concepts such as movement, interaction, and basic combat.

Zone 0 is much smaller than the other Zones and devoid of all life aside from The Judge, Sugar, Valérie (formerly),[4] and later Zacharie. The reason for Zone 0's relative lack of life given the abundance of Elsen in the other Zones is unclear.

Items and Interactables A Luck Ticket and Silver Flesh are found in chests outside the main building. Various memory puzzles that are controlled through the use of yellow cubes. A red save cube is found behind the main building.

Trivia Zone 0 is the only zone that's never seen purified by the Batter. It's possible that it was purified offscreen as a result of the Batter killing the Judge in one of the endings, since the Judge is the guardian of Zone 0. Zone 0 is the only zone that's ever seen in one color through the simplistic tileset used almost everywhere else in OFF; it is never seen in a purified state anywhere, and its color does not change from yellow even when going indoors, in contrast to the other zones, which have at least more than one palette (spring green/purple, pink/blue, orange/green, black/red) Zone 0 has a chapter dedicated to it in Explanations.
