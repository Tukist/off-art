Spectres are the primary enemy of OFF . They are hostile towards entities who are not also spectres, and terrorize the residents of each Zone . They come in many shapes and sizes, although they share similarities in their appearances. One of The Batter 's main objectives is to eradicate the spectres from each Zone.

It is implied by The Judge that The Queen is the only one who has control over the spectres. Japhet , at one point, also insists that he can control them, although he is shown to fail. The Judge also asserts that Japhet cannot control them, despite his claims. Not only can the Queen control the Spectres, but according to a book in the library, she may even have the ability to create them and may also be the one sending out the Spectres to destroy the land that she created, however, the book ends by saying that "those rumors are nothing but speculations". A piece of evidence that supports this is the fact that Japhet (possessing Valerie 's body) states that "they are my arms, sent to me by the Queen to restore justice to this zone".

### Trivia
- Nine out of the twenty enemies shown above are not technically spectres. January, Tiburce, Troquantary, Arpagon, Gilles de Rais, Massu, and Von Gacy are listed as ghouls in their flavor text. Magnolia and Crown of the Dead, meanwhile, are listed as "head" and "crown" respectively in the translated files. All other enemies are listed as spectres, either in their names and/or in their files.
