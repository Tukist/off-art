The Nothingness serves as the main hub of the game, as the Player can access any Zone and the Room through it. The green areas appear to be an aerial view of the zones and the Room. It can be entered through using red save boxes.

It consists of 3 green structures, with hilighted pixels inside them that lead to the zones and The Room when interacted with, if you have an adequate zodiac card. On the left, the text "world map (?)" floats by.

Trivia
The song "Silencio" plays in the background while in the Nothingness. It consists of whispers, murmurs and hushing all happening at once. The whispers and murmurs seem to be spoken in French.
