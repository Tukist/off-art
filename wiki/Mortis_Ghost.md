# Mortis Ghost

Mortis Ghost as depicted in the end credits of DUPLO.
Martin Georis (born 1987), also known as Mortis Ghost, is a Belgian comic artist and game developer. He is most well-known for creating the critically-acclaimed surreal RPG Maker game OFF along with his friend Alias Conrad Coldwood. The game was initially released on May 14, 2008, and a remake of the game was developed with Fangamer and released on August 15, 2025.

Trivia
He generally posts in French unless he's talking to an English-speaking fan. In audio interviews, he has spoken fluently in both French and English. He has also streamed a discussion of how OFF was made for its 10th and 15th anniversary in English.
His art style leans into surrealism and usually utilizes round, yet abstract shapes.

In the late 2000's, they appeared geometric and loose with more shading and detail, seemingly to be drawn with contour lines and have a rough texture. For battle sprites, he scanned his sketches and polished them for a lower scale (as downscaling them would make them unreadable).
At an unknown point, he commonly utilizes colors in his art (even in subtle ways, like vividly colored backgrounds against monochrome characters), textured lineart (similar to ink) and more rounded, simpler designs. Shading is flat, sometimes subtle, and sometimes warm colors will be used.
In contrast to his old sprites, the pillar art has no anti-aliasing, likely for readability.

He created OFF while studying comic art at the Institut Saint-Luc in Brussels, Belgium.[1]
He finished making OFF in 2007 after a year of development. He has stated that the game was made for fun as a hobby, and that the process of making it "did not have very high stakes." The most difficult part of the process was patching bugs after its initial release.[1]
Pieces for OFF tend to be monochrome, and he has published plenty of concept sketches for OFF on his accounts which can be seen on the characters' galleries.
