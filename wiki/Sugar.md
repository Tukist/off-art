### Profile
#### Appearance
Sugar has short hair and a dark shadow around her eyes, which almost appear to be empty. She wears an open shirt with X's on her chest, and baggy pants. Her sleeves and socks have a stripe pattern at the ends. Dummie heads are seen dangling from her hands, which are revealed through use of the Batter's Wide Angle to be made of candy.

Sugar has been confirmed by Mortis Ghost to be a human.

#### Personality
Sugar's name and attacks heavily imply some sort of fondness for sweets, as do the piles of sugar seen in the room she's encountered in and the item she drops upon her defeat. Her odd behavior makes her a very enigmatic character. She speaks in a rather melancholy, simple, and strange manner, and laughs during her dialogue, similar to Zacharie. She is also somewhat aloof, outright ignoring the Batter's questions and refusing to shed light on herself via "useless introductions". In a way, she seems to be broken by the world and withdrawn, as she believes that everything is frightening.

### In Battle
Sugar is the strongest boss in the game, with a max HP of 12,000. She is, however, extremely susceptible to poison, nullifying any difficulty. She also has four Dummie heads stuck on hooks, two on each hand, and one candy on each hook. Defeating her will drop the Grand Chocolatier, which can then be given to Zacharie in Zone 0 (with the rest of elements) to receive either the Aries-Card or Ashley Bat.

### Relationships
#### Zacharie
It is heavily implied that she has some sort of personal relationship with Zacharie. After defeating her, she asks the Batter to tell Zacharie goodbye for her, shortly before dying. Talking to Zacharie after defeating her will yield a simple, solemn response, his usual laugh gone quiet.

### Trivia
- "Hard-bitten Caramel" is Sugar's only move that is translated into English (the original French is "Caramel Dur"). It was renamed to "Caramel Durr" in the 2025 remaster.
- Sugar sees the Batter as a "frightening duck", which might be a reference to the Batter's form in the Judge's special ending, and it also leads some suspicions that we see him differently than other characters in the game. It's possible that she may just be referring to the Batter himself, as the tip of a Baseball hat is referred to as a bill. Interestingly, there is a swan pattern on the floor in the room where Sugar resides.
- Using the Wide Angle or Eye item will display a window with the implication that Sugar has no weaknesses nor resistances, in spite of the fact that she's been confirmed to be susceptible to poison.
- When fans brought up the possibility of Sugar being transgender, Mortis Ghost replied with "why not?"
